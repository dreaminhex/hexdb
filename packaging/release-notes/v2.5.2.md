HexDB 2.5.2 fixes signing in to the admin UI with a browser's saved password.

## Fixed

- **Sign-in with autofill.** The Sign in button stayed disabled until you typed or clicked in a field, because a browser fills saved logins without telling the page until it's touched. The button is now always active (empty fields are still refused), and sign-in reads what the fields hold when it's sent.
- **macOS installer.** The DMG is sized from its contents; with 2.5's larger binaries, `hdiutil`'s own estimate ran short.

## Upgrading

Restart the hexes one at a time, as usual: replicas first, the Overseer last. Nothing else changes.
