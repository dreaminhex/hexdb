HexDB 2.1.1 renames sharding to slicing: the 64 parts a lattice's data is split into are now called slices, in the settings, the API, the dashboard and the documentation.

## Changed

- **Settings:** `replication.sharding` is now `replication.slicing`, and `replication.shard_copies` is now `replication.slice_copies`. The old names are no longer read, so a lattice that had sharding on must use the new names (with them missing, replicas keep full copies again, which is safe). Environment variables: `HEXDB_REPLICATION__SLICING`, `HEXDB_REPLICATION__SLICE_COPIES`.
- **Status:** `/status` reports `replication.slices_held_count` and `replication.sliced_documents`, and the lattice view's hexes have `slicing` and `slices_held`.
- **Errors:** a replica holding slices answers live-feed requests with 421 `sliced_replica`.
- **Wording:** the in-memory Reed-Solomon pieces are called fragments in the dashboard, logs and documentation. The JSON field names of the vertex and scrub reports don't change.

## Upgrading a lattice

Upgrade hexes one at a time as usual. 2.1.1 hexes understand 2.1.0 hexes' announcements during the upgrade, and replicas keep the slices they already hold.
