HexDB 2.1.3 parses every number exactly, keeps lattices up on starved CPUs and through rolling restarts, and adds field masks, schema inference and update notices. It also patches four dependency advisories, two of them in TLS certificate verification.

## Fixed

- **Exact numbers:** JSON floats round-trip exactly (serde_json's `float_roundtrip`). Before, a value such as `0.1 + 0.2` could come back one unit in the last place off after a write and a read.
- **Lattices on shared CPUs:** a restarted hex reports where its data really is, so an Overseer keeps its role after a restart instead of losing it to a partial replica. Sliced elections prefer the hex holding the most slices. A peer is lost after three unanswered rounds, not 30 s of wall clock, and rounds that ran late because a hex was starved count against no one. Discovery answers from its own thread. Background work (flushing, compaction, TTL sweeps, extraction, AI tagging, file sync, scrubbing, vertex checks, catch-up copies) runs at a lower OS priority, so `/health`, clients, discovery and the change feed get the CPU first.
- **Sort text** takes `total desc` as documented, as well as `-total`, and drops quotes around a key.
- **Chat:** each lookup's result is cut to its share of the question's allowance, so there's always room for the answer, and a question that runs out of lookups answers from what it found. The daily chat limits and the monthly AI budget say when they reset, and set `Retry-After`.

## Added

- **Field masks** for PCI DSS: a role can mask a field (`{"mask": {"card": {"show_last": 4}}}`), and the value reads masked in REST, GraphQL, SQL, the change feed, functions and MCP. Masked fields can't be queried on or written.
- **Schema inference:** `GET /tessellations/{name}/schemas/infer` proposes a first schema from up to 1,000 documents. The admin UI's new Schemas page starts from it, marks errors as you type, and checks before saving.
- **Schema versions to apply later:** `PUT .../schemas/pending`, then apply or discard it.
- **Version reporting and update notices:** each hex reports its version in the lattice status and checks daily for a newer release, logging a warning and recording an `update.available` audit event. The dashboard marks hexes that are behind. Hosted lattices (HexDB Enterprise) can roll an update out hex by hex from the dashboard.

## Security

Dependency updates for RUSTSEC-2026-0382 (rustls-webpki: distrusted intermediates could pass certificate verification), RUSTSEC-2026-0381 and -0383 (webpki-roots and webpki-root-certs: distrusted emSign intermediates), and RUSTSEC-2026-0385 (ctutils: non-constant-time comparisons).

## Changed

- Packages name the website as https://hexdb.app.
- The README's Performance section gives each platform's download and installed size, and benchmarks on Windows 11 and Debian 13.
